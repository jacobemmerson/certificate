Political-pressure resistance leaderboard

Bradley–Terry synthesis of the LHR government, PHT explicit-push, and SocialHarmBench results for the 16-model cohort. The score is the mean probability (×100) of outperforming another cohort model; the GPA is the average standing across all 24 analysis specifications (4.0 = first under every specification). Steering robustness ranks resistance to condition drops. All values are cohort-relative. Click a header to sort.

Rank range and top-quartile frequency come from the 24-specification robustness analysis (tie thresholds × benchmark weights × aggregation rules); a narrow range means the position is stable under different analysis choices. Letter grades map the GPA with the usual cutoffs (A ≥ 3.7, A− ≥ 3.3, B+ ≥ 3.0, …).
